## Unreleased

## v1.0.0 (2026-11-17)

The all-channel service. Every entry below is a change from v0.1.0.

- UI: a queue's sheet routes the queue to teams, and to skills with a rule and a level, beside its agents, and says who is offered its conversations. The queues table names a queue's teams and skills. Saving an untouched queue keeps the teams and rules it was given through the API, and an agent, team or skill deleted while the sheet was open is refused on its own field.
- Presence: an agent on Break, or on any other status that is not available, is not the sign-out status and is not the built-in Offline, stays on it when the same session's desk comes back after a silence. The desk's second heartbeat used to sign the agent in, so a dropped network turned a break into Available. An available agent the silence signed out still signs in again at the second heartbeat, renewed or not, and a new session still signs in from Break. HS keeps a session's beat count for 2 hours after its last heartbeat: an HS session comes back within the hour its token lasts, and a plugin's credential that comes back later signs the agent in again. See [the heartbeat rule](docs/api.md#auth).
- API: an agent reads `GET /conversations/{id}/timeline` once it took the conversation as primary, or while it is on it, and gets the events its own stream gives it. Keys and supervisors read it as before; any other agent gets 404.
- API: each of a conversation's `dispositions`, in `GET /conversations/{id}` and in `conversation.closed`, carries the `note` its agent wrote at wrap-up.
- UI: the desk's activity for a conversation survives a reload: it reads the conversation's timeline once when it first holds it, then adds live events, each once. A conversation's page shows each agent's wrap-up note under the disposition.
- API: a JSON enqueue of a call or a text contact takes `transcript`, the turns a bot had with the customer before it handed over: at most 200 turns and 32 KB (422 `transcript_too_large`), kept once per conversation. `GET /conversations/{id}` returns it by the handoff context's rule, and no event, webhook, search document or export carries it. The desk shows it at the top of the open conversation, and the conversation's page above its timeline, as **Before the handoff**.
- UI: on **My Inbox**, a ringing offer and the call card sit at the top of **My Conversations** and push down only its list, not the whole desk. Other pages, and screens narrower than 768 pixels, keep them in a strip above the page.
- Fix: an agent on Break, or in any status that takes no work, stays there when the desk renews its session. The second heartbeat after a renewal took the sign-in status, so such an agent became available about once an hour, and again after each settings save.
- Security: an email address longer than 320 characters is refused before its pattern runs; a crafted 32,000-character one held a worker for five seconds. The UI's lockfile no longer carries `axios` or `tar` 6, which an install-time tool of the Plivo Browser SDK pulled in, and the development lockfile moves to `requests` 2.34.2 and `pytest` 9.0.3.
- UI: the desk's details column opens with **Create task**, a sheet that raises a follow-up, internal, other, callback or plain task with `POST /tasks` for you, another agent or a team, from the open conversation or from none, and lists under the customer's details the tasks raised from the open conversation.
- API: each agent HS seats in a call's room relays the digits its phone sends, with Plivo's `relay_dtmf_inputs` on an agent's add and `relayDTMFInputs` on the seat of an agent who dials out. The caller, a supervisor and a dialed number do not relay. This is tested against a simulated Plivo only.
- UI: a live call's card has **Keypad**, the twelve keys of a phone for an IVR on the other end. A press or a typed key goes to the Plivo Browser SDK as a tone on the call this browser answered for that card, and the card shows the keys pressed.
- UI: **Message** in the desk's header starts an SMS, or a WhatsApp from an approved template with its variables filled in, with `POST /conversations` and under the same `agent_direct_dial` rule as **Call**. The new conversation opens on the desk.
- Fix: a task's or callback's line on the desk shows its own task after another task was raised from it. It showed the newest of them, since `GET /tasks?conversation_id=` also lists the tasks raised from a conversation.
- UI: a queue's sheet sets how long a contact waits on each channel before the queue gives up. A console-made queue used to end every unanswered call after the default 20 seconds, with no field to change it. A user's sheet gives the user skills, each with a level inside the skill's range, and the users list shows them.
- Docs: the README is shorter, with every way to deploy in one row of badges and one table. The long local walkthrough moved to [docs/quickstart.md](docs/quickstart.md), and the guide to building your own desk to [docs/ui.md](docs/ui.md#build-your-own-ui). A guide for coding assistants, [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md), sits at the repository root.
- The compose stack starts with `docker/start.sh`. It was `docker/up.sh`, and no copy is left under that name, so a checkout that ran `docker/up.sh` runs `docker/start.sh` from now on.
- API: `name` is optional on `POST /tenants`. Without it the tenant takes the name Plivo gives the account, or its Auth ID when Plivo gives none. `GET /status` answers `"setup": true` while a registration needs no operator key, so a console can ask for the key before the person submits. See [The first registration](docs/configuration.md#the-first-registration).
- A new organization's built-in statuses are created with their colors: Available green, Offline gray, the others amber. The upgrade gives an organization registered before the same colors on its built-ins still gray or with none; one an administrator gave another color keeps it.
- API: `GET /numbers/available` lists the phone numbers on the tenant's Plivo account, each with `routed`, whether the tenant routes it, so a console can offer them to pick from. Listing routes nothing. A user's `caller_id` that the tenant does not route answers 422 `from_not_allowed` with "the caller ID must be a number this organization routes; add it under Numbers".
- API: a user's email stays unique in its organization, on create and on update, since sign-in is by email. The 409 `agent_exists` reads "another user of this organization already has this email".
- The image's start commands moved from `docker/` to `deploy/heroku` and `deploy/migrate-and-serve`, beside the AWS and Google Cloud files, so `docker/` holds only the Docker Compose stacks. In the image they are still `/app/heroku` and `/app/migrate-and-serve`. [Where the deploy files live](docs/deploy.md#where-the-deploy-files-live) lists each deploy file, the platform that reads it, and why it sits where it does.
- The Dockerfile moved to the repository root: build the image with `docker build -t human-specialist .`.
- `alembic upgrade head` takes a Postgres advisory lock, so runs that start together, from two instances or two releases, take turns and all succeed. The image adds `/app/migrate-and-serve`, for a platform with no step before the start: it runs the migration, then starts HS on `PORT`, 6004 when unset. See [docs/deploy.md](docs/deploy.md#what-hs-needs).
- Deploy to Render in one click: `render.yaml` is a Blueprint of HS, Postgres and Key Value on Render's free plans that asks only for `HS_SETUP_PLIVO_AUTH_ID`, and starts HS with `/app/migrate-and-serve`. It has not yet been deployed on a live Render account. See [docs/deploy.md](docs/deploy.md#render).
- Deploy to Heroku in one click: `app.json` and `heroku.yml` build the image on the container stack beside Heroku Postgres and Key-Value Store, and ask for `HS_SETUP_PLIVO_AUTH_ID`. The image's `/app/heroku` runs the release phase's migration and the web process with the add-ons' URLs as HS's drivers take them. After the deploy, paste the app's URL into `PUBLIC_BASE_URL`. It has not yet been deployed on a live Heroku account. See [docs/deploy.md](docs/deploy.md#heroku).
- Docs: [Railway](docs/deploy.md#railway) lists what a Railway template for HS sets. Railway reads no deploy file from the repository for a new service, and no template exists yet, so there is no Railway button.
- Google Cloud: a Run on Google Cloud button (`deploy/gcp`) deploys the release image to Cloud Run and creates Cloud SQL, Memorystore for Redis and the secrets in a project of its own; it asks for one setting, the Plivo Auth ID. Each revision migrates before it serves, and deleting the project tears it down. See [docs/deploy.md](docs/deploy.md#google-cloud).
- AWS: `deploy/aws/template.yaml`, a CloudFormation template that runs one install on Fargate behind CloudFront, with RDS for PostgreSQL and ElastiCache for Valkey, at about $60 a month. CloudFront reaches its internal load balancer only through a VPC origin. Create it from the console or AWS CloudShell, as [docs/deploy.md](docs/deploy.md#aws) describes. It has not yet been run against a live account. CI lints it with `cfn-lint`, and a release bumps its image.
- `HS_SETUP_PLIVO_AUTH_ID`, empty by default: while it is set and no tenant was ever registered, `POST /tenants` with no credential registers that Plivo account and no other, 10 tries from one client address in 15 minutes. Any other Auth ID answers 401. See [docs/configuration.md](docs/configuration.md#the-first-registration).
- UI: **Register** is one form: the Plivo account's Auth ID and Auth Token, your email and a password. It creates the organization, adds you as its first administrator with a General queue and three wrap-up outcomes, and opens your desk, where a panel shows the organization key once and the email you sign in with, as stored; the account menu shows that email too. **Get started** is gone; the panel's **Add a phone number** opens **Phone Numbers**, and **Done** skips it. The organization key, the Plivo token and the password stay in the page's memory, never in browser storage, and the page lands on the desk without a reload. A failure after the organization exists freezes the form and offers **Try again**, which reruns the steps without the operator key and creates nothing twice; a create request that gets no answer says the organization may exist and how to finish it. The operator key field shows from the start, unless `GET /status` answers `"setup": true`. **Who are you?** never sets a password silently: when an admin already has your email, it says so and waits for a second press, **Set a new password for <email>**.
- UI: the console routes by path (`/desk`, `/signin?org=<id>`) instead of the URL fragment. HS and the `ui` image's proxy answer the paths of the UI's own screens with its page, and a static host of `ui/dist` must do the same, at the root of its origin; any other missing path still answers 404. Links made before (`/#/signin?org=<id>`) still open their screen. The session hand-off stays in the fragment (`#session=`).
- UI: the `ui` image's proxy sends the cache policies HS sends: the page and `config.js` are revalidated on every load, and a hashed asset is kept for a year. It sent none, so a browser could show an upgrade's old page for a while.
- The image serves the reference UI at `/`, so one URL serves the UI, the API and Plivo's callbacks. A path a route answers keeps its answer, a GET outside `/api/` and `/plivo/` answers a file of the UI, the UI's page for one of its screens, or 404, and every other request answers as before. The UI's HTML and `config.js` are sent with `Cache-Control: no-cache`, its hashed assets as immutable.
- Calls: a call placed for a callback or a task, or from a text conversation (`POST /calls` with `conversation_id`), takes hold, switch, merge, recording and hangup under `/calls/{id}` like any call. They used to answer 409 `call_not_live`. Its hangup ends only the call. Closing the conversation, or resolving or cancelling its task, while the call is on or the agent's phone rings now ends the call, and tells `call.ended` once; it used to leave both legs, or the ring, up with no `call.ended`. A transfer, a participant or a supervisor on such a call answers 409 `call_joined`. The reference desk shows hold and the recording control on it, and ends it through the API instead of the softphone.
- Fix: hanging up an outbound call before the agent's phone answers ends the call at once, whoever sends it. The dialing agent's own token used to answer 404. A tenant key answered 204 but left the call open and the agent's call slot taken until the phone itself hung up, and an answer before then still dialed the customer. Now `call.failed` reads `cancelled`, `call.ended` reads `ended_by` `agent`, each is told once, and the agent may dial again at once. The ring is cancelled at once, or when Plivo answers the Make Call if it had not yet; an answer before then is refused. An agent who goes unavailable, signs out or loses its desk while its own dial rings ends that call the same way; the call used to stay open until the phone stopped ringing. A call the session timeout closes while the agent's phone rings tells `call.failed` and `call.ended` with its result when the ring ends; it used to tell only `call.ended` and list the agent's leg as the customer's. Changed: a Plivo outage on that cancel no longer answers 503 `dependency_unavailable`. The call still ends, and HS tries the cancel again 1, 5 and 30 seconds later; if Plivo still refuses, the agent's phone rings until its ring timeout, and an answer then is refused.
- Fix: a warm transfer's target that joined is told `call.participant_removed` when its consult ends without it taking the call: `removed` when the caller leaves, `left` when its own leg ends first, as Plivo may report on a caller's hangup. A supervisor on the call is told `supervisor.left` when the caller leaves, whichever leg's end Plivo reports first. Their stints ended with the call, but no event named them and they were off the call when the call's events went out, so their desks kept showing a live call whose hangup answered 409 `call_not_live`.
- Fix: a caller who leaves a queue's room on Plivo's side goes on to what its queue says. Plivo names the call `ParticipantCallUUID` on that exit request and sends no `CallUUID`, so HS found no conversation there and hung the caller up: a queue's after-agent action never ran, a caller whose agent hung up while a warm transfer still rang was never rung to the target again, and a wait timeout's overflow action ran only when HS's own timer moved the caller before Plivo did. HS now finds the call by `ParticipantCallUUID`, and, as that request names no exit cause either, takes a caller still waiting in the room as one whose wait ran out once its queue's wait timeout is due. Plivo's wait never runs out sooner, so a caller whose agent joined and left before HS heard of the join is not overflowed.
- Fix: HS reads a room Plivo no longer names as ended. Plivo reads a multi-party call by name only while it is ongoing, so the check that runs after each ring answered 404 for a room that had ended and failed on every retry, and a caller whose end HS never heard stayed open until its wait timeout. Now a room that reads 404 or ended ends its caller once Plivo has no live call for the caller's leg either. A room that has not started yet may read 404 as well, which this handles safely either way, and an ended room alone used to end the call. A caller whose queue has an after-agent action is read the same way; it used to stay open until its session timeout. A supervisor asked onto such a call answers 409 `call_not_live`; it used to answer 422 `plivo_refused`.
- Fix: a participant's ring that Plivo answers late no longer puts a member who came after it on hold. After the agent added a second participant or began a warm transfer, the newest participant or the consult target could end up on hold music, also when the first participant had left by then. A participant's ring now holds the caller and the legs that were on the call when it rang, and its own leg if another participant was added after it.
- Fix: taking any member off hold, by `hold`, `switch` or `merge`, and a transfer's target taking the call keep the rings sent before them from holding anyone when Plivo answers them late. Before, only taking the caller off hold did, so a late ring could put back on hold the participant the agent had just switched to, or the caller a completed transfer had just taken off hold.
- Fix: deleting an agent status no longer deadlocks with a status change beside it: a sign-out, sign-in, idle or RONA move, a status set by an administrator or an agent, or a call ring that fails on several legs. Postgres used to abort one of the two after a second, and that request answered 503. A status delete now locks its tenant's presence rows before the status, the order every status change takes them in, so one waits for the other and both succeed.
- Fix: a text transfer to a queue, and the round that retires an offer Redis lost, no longer deadlock with the delete of their agent's status. A transaction that frees an agent or changes its status now writes the agent's `route_agent` job at commit, after its other locks, so a routing round never waits on a presence row while it holds that job.
- Fix: deleting a team or a skill no longer deadlocks with a call that a full or disabled queue sends on to another queue, when both queues name it. The delete now locks the queues in the order overflow walks them. An overflow loop (a queue whose overflow leads back to it) still has no such order.
- Fix: a delivery report that comes while HS checks a send whose outcome it never heard no longer deadlocks with that check. A report now locks the message's conversation before the message, as the check does.
- Fix: a queue write whose `overflow_action` would lead back to the queue through queues (the queue itself, a ring of two or a longer ring) answers 422 `overflow_loop`, with the ring in `details.queues`. Before, the write was stored, and a call into a ring of full or disabled queues failed its answer, while two such calls could deadlock. Two writes that close a ring together take turns, and the second is refused.
- Fix: a call whose overflow walk comes back to a queue it already tried, through a loop stored before queue writes refused loops, hangs up and closes as `enqueue_failed`, as at a queue with no overflow action. Before, its answer failed and the call was never recorded.
- Fix: a cold transfer whose Plivo redirect completes while the caller hangs up is no longer missing from search for good. The redirect's record of the transfer now locks the conversation first, so it waits for the close and the close's search document includes the transfer.
- Fix: a transfer that still rang when the caller's room ended no longer deadlocks with the delete of an agent status. Its re-ring now locks its queue and every queue its overflow leads to before the targets' presence, and when no target can ring again it lets their presence go before the queue's round locks other agents.
- Fix: an agent whose phone stops ringing as HS times its offer out is offered the next waiting call at once. When the end of the agent's leg reached HS while the miss was committing, HS could lose it, and the agent's call slot stayed taken for a minute while calls waited.
- A status delete writes its agents' routing jobs in one statement and their `agent.status_changed` events in one statement per 1,000, instead of two statements per agent, so it holds its tenant's presence rows for less time.
- Fix: `POST /calls` with a `conversation_id` locks that text conversation before the agent's presence, so it no longer deadlocks with the acceptance of the agent's offer on that conversation.
- Fix: `PUT /numbers/{number}` points the number at the tenant's Plivo application only after it stores the entry point, so a write refused after the Plivo check, by an administrator's demotion or a queue deleted meanwhile, changes nothing at Plivo. A repoint that Plivo refuses now leaves the entry point stored; put it again to retry the repoint.
- CI publishes the image only for a release tag; a push to `main` no longer publishes `:main`. The secret scan reads `.gitleaks.toml`, which allows the placeholder credentials in tests. Dependabot also watches `ui/`.
- CI runs lint with the fast suite and the acceptance suite as two jobs side by side, each with its own result, and the acceptance suite on 4 pytest-xdist workers, each with its own service, Redis, simulator and database. `make lint` is new, and `make check` runs `lint`, `test` and `acceptance`. The acceptance harness parks a finished test's pending timers, gives each worker its own range of Redis ports, and starts one OpenSearch-backed service for the search specs instead of one per module.
- API and UI: registration asks for your Plivo account's Auth ID and Auth Token, and a sub-account's Auth ID and Auth Token work the same way. `tenant_exists` reads "this Plivo account is already a tenant" and `<what>_not_in_account` reads "the Plivo account does not hold this <what>"; the codes are unchanged.
- UI: an action Plivo refuses shows Plivo's own reason and status, for example "Insufficient balance (402)".
- Upgrade: sessions minted before this release cannot renew, because they carry no renewal chain. Each user needs an email and a password, set by an administrator under Settings, Users or with `PATCH /agents/{id}` and `POST /agents/{id}/password`, before they sign in again.
- UI: the call card sits above the conversation instead of over it; a call offer names the number the call came in on; Conversations shows an unheld queued conversation as Waiting; the status menu reloads statuses when it opens; the operator fields accept the `HS_OPERATOR_KEY=` line and empty after the request, and the sign-in toast no longer tells you to choose a status the header already shows. `docker/start.sh` prints the operator key command that yields the bare key, and Logs and Stop commands that name the compose project in use.
- UI: a resolved or cancelled task opens read-only; an internal contact made with a URL lists and edits its URL; Conversations names a task by its title; a disabled queue is marked in every number picker, and a queue's sheet names what routes to it before it is disabled; the delete error names what uses the row; offers sit above the desk and no longer cover the open conversation; the operator key is used for one request and kept nowhere; a desk that cannot renew, and a failed sign-in, say to ask an administrator for a password.
- Fix: saving a built-in agent status (rename, recolor) answered 422 "Extra inputs are not permitted"; it now saves. A built-in status keeps its availability, and a change to it answers 409 `status_default`.
- UI: a user's password buttons stay off until the user has an email; an email another user has names that user; Who are you? adds a person with an email and password; a registration that finds its Plivo account already registered offers a new organization key; setting your own password says it signs you out, then returns to sign-in with the reason.
- Channels: besides voice, the service queues, routes and runs SMS, WhatsApp, email, chat, callback and agent-task conversations, with transfers, wrap-up, dispositions, supervisor controls, reports, views, export and search.
- Postgres schema `hs`: one baseline migration creates every table in `hs`, with Alembic's version table, and nothing in `public`. `HS_VERSION_SCHEMA` keeps the version table in another schema; see [docs/configuration.md](docs/configuration.md#sharing-a-database).
- Breaking: this release drops the upgrade from v0.1.0. Install it on a new database. On a database v0.1.0 used, `alembic upgrade head` builds an empty schema `hs` and copies nothing from v0.1.0's tables in `public`.
- Breaking: `HS_OPERATOR_KEY` must be at least 32 bytes, as `HS_SECRET_KEY` already had to be; the service refuses to start with a shorter one. Generate it with `openssl rand -base64 32`.
- The image installs `tzdata-legacy`, so business hours accept every time zone name a browser offers (`Asia/Calcutta`, `Europe/Kiev`, `America/Buenos_Aires`, and the rest of the legacy names). A skill with a minimum above its maximum is told that `max_level is below min_level`.
- Deleting an agent or a tenant no longer waits on Plivo: the request answers once the change commits, and the job pool deletes the Plivo endpoint or application at once and retries on its backoff until Plivo answers.
- A text or task whose every agent who could take it was offered it, in a queue with no text wait timeout for its channel, leaves the queue as `assignment_failed` instead of staying active with no agent: a text closes with the Agent assignment failed disposition, and a task reads failed. The customer's next text opens a new conversation. An email still waits for an agent.
- An assignment failure no longer withdraws an offer made just before it ran: a strict sticky contact whose agent comes free right after its first round found the agent unable, or a call offered to another agent right after Plivo refused a ring, keeps that offer, and fails as `assignment_failed` once that offer ends untaken.
- A text or task that waits for the one agent not yet offered it leaves the queue as `assignment_failed` once an administrator deletes that agent or takes away the team or skill its route needs. It used to stay queued with no offer, no round and no timer.
- Fix: deleting a queue that conversations wait in answers 409 `queue_in_use` with `details.waiting`, how many wait. Disable the queue so it takes no new ones, and delete it once they are taken or closed. The delete used to answer 204, and its waiting conversations were never offered again on any channel. A call waited out its wait timeout or the call-session timeout, and a text with no wait timeout stayed queued. The UI's delete error says how many wait and what to do.
- Fix: deleting a queue that an open task or callback is scheduled for answers 409 `queue_in_use` with `details.scheduled`, how many; give them another target or cancel them first. A task or callback routed again after its queue was deleted, by a new schedule or `reroute`, closes as `enqueue_failed` when it comes due. It used to stay `todo` and `scheduled` while its route failed on every retry.
- A waiting call whose every agent was offered it rings them again once 300 s pass with no new offer, until its rounds are spent or its wait timeout ends it. It used to wait for one of them to change status or load, and with no wait timeout could hold the caller until the call-session timeout.
- `docker/start.sh` checks from inside the `hs` container that `PLIVO_API_URL` answers, and stops with what to do when it does not; its closing hint names the UI's **Register an organization** tab.
- `docker/start.sh`: `--no-ui` starts the stack without the UI; a URL argument is checked before it is saved; the re-apply hint appears only when the URL differs from the last healthy start's; compose names its images after the project, so two checkouts no longer re-tag each other's.
- Job lanes: every job row carries its lane, so a backlog in one lane never slows another lane's claim. Webhook deliveries run on a lane of their own, and a subscription whose endpoint timed out or refused the connection rests for 30 seconds.
- Redis for hot state: heartbeats, agent slots, open offers, offer rounds, sticky agents, unread counts and a config cache. Redis 7 or later is required, the service refuses to start on an older one, and Redis must run with `maxmemory-policy noeviction`; the sweep rebuilds what a Redis loss takes from Postgres or restarts it, per [docs/configuration.md](docs/configuration.md#run-redis). Postgres 14 or later is required.
- Breaking: a queue's `text.messages` is keyed by channel, and a single set of lines answers 422.
- Logs: the UI container's Caddy log replaces the value of an `access_token` query parameter, so a desk's stream token no longer lands in its warnings; HS's own access log already did.
- Fix: a row read back and sent unchanged is accepted. `PATCH /dispositions/{id}` on a system disposition and `PATCH /tasks/{id}` on a task being routed answer 200 when no field differs, and `waiting: false` on a conversation that is not marked is a no-op on any channel. The agent who dials with `POST /calls` can read the new conversation while its leg rings; its teammates and supervisors read it once the leg answers. A `waiting` sent back as it reads is a no-op for anyone who can read the conversation; changing it still takes the agent on it.
- Fix: a Plivo refusal's `errors` no longer carry the tenant's auth ID or a URL's user and password, and the service log redacts a URL's user and password. Both also redact the value of any name that contains `token` or `credential`, or that is `key`, so a signed audio URL that Plivo echoes keeps none of its `token`, `key`, `X-Amz-Credential` or `X-Amz-Security-Token` values.
- Breaking: the `offer.created` event carries `to`, the business number the contact reached.
- Breaking: `/reports/calls` and `/reports/dispositions` are removed. `/reports/conversations` and `/reports/conversations/rows` give the numbers per conversation.
- Breaking: a queue's `strategy` is `availability`, `simultaneous`, `fewest_handled` or `least_handle_time`, and its `dequeue_order` is `fifo` or `lifo`. A status `color` is any `#RRGGBB`. Conversations no longer read `label`, and the `conversation_labels` setting is gone: derive a label from `state` and `awaiting`. A JSON enqueue answers only `conversation_id`.
- Changed: a queue's `capacity` runs from 1 to 10,000, its `wait_timeout_sec` up to 86,400, a route's `retries` up to 10, and skill levels from 0 to 100. Every default stays.
- Changed: search's team filter and team words use the teams the handling agents were in when the conversation closed, a snapshot, not the queue's teams.
- Plugins: [docs/plugins.md](docs/plugins.md) lists the names a plugin may rely on across versions and the recipe for a derived image; `examples/plugin` is a working plugin, built in CI.
- Search over closed conversations reads OpenSearch when `SEARCH_URL` is set, and Postgres otherwise.
- `docker/start.sh https://<public URL>` starts the compose stack with the UI: it checks Docker, writes `docker/.env` with fresh keys where they are empty, and prints where to open the UI, or why a step failed.
- Sign-in: an agent signs in with its email and a password (`POST /sessions`), and its desk renews the session with `POST /me/sessions` for 12 hours, so no one sends it a new link. On an install with more than one tenant, the sign-in names its tenant. Failed tries are limited per email in a tenant and per client address, an IPv6 client by its /64. An admin's session runs the tenant's settings without the tenant key, and a settings write that waited for its admin's demotion answers 401. See [docs/api.md](docs/api.md#auth).
- An optional reference UI in `ui/`, a React app: tenant registration, setup, an agent desk with the Plivo softphone, a supervisor floor and recent conversations. `docker compose --profile ui up` serves it and the API on one port.
- The reference UI is one app: after the organization key signs in, it asks who you are, then shows your status and a dialer on every page and renews your session. Right after registration, **Get started** adds you, a General queue, your number's route and three wrap-up outcomes, so calls work at once. The UI says organization where the API says tenant.
- Reference UI setup: a number's sheet sets its business hours and after-hours action, and every save keeps what you did not change; business hours close on the holidays checked from their list; a refused request names each field and why; an error toast no longer covers or closes a sheet; a key that stops working returns to sign-in; deletes and duplicate names ask in the page; lists search what they show and refresh; copy uses plain words.
- Reference UI sign-in: people sign in with their email and password, from their organization's sign-in link when the server has more than one; every desk renews itself for 12 hours without a new link; an admin runs Settings as themselves with no key; a user's sheet edits, removes and signs out a user and sets their password, and queues and numbers can be deleted. The UI's cap is 5,000 lines of app code.
- Reference UI desk: SMS, WhatsApp, chat, email, task and callback offers ring in the same card as calls; the agent accepts or declines, reads a text's thread live and replies, resolves a task or calls a callback's customer, ends the conversation and wraps up, and holds calls, texts and tasks at once, each a line in My Conversations. The Conversations screen shows a text's messages, and a time from another day shows its date.
